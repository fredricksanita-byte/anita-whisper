# Anita Whisper

Don't type. Just talk. Free voice typing for Windows that runs on your own computer.

**Download:** https://fredricksanita-byte.github.io/anita-whisper/

Press F8, talk, press F8 again, and your words are typed wherever your cursor is.
Speech is turned into text on your computer; nothing is sent over the internet.

This repository holds the download page. The installer is attached to the
[latest release](https://github.com/fredricksanita-byte/anita-whisper/releases/latest).
