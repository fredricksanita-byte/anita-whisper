ANITA WHISPER - Don't type. Just talk.

HOW TO USE
1. Click where you want to type (an email, a text box, a document).
2. Press F8. A black-and-red bubble says "Listening...".
3. Talk normally.
4. Press F8 again. Your words are typed where your cursor is.

Close Anita Whisper:  press Ctrl + Alt + F8
Open it again:        double-click the Anita Whisper icon, or find it in the Start menu.

SPELL YOUR NAMES RIGHT
Start menu > Anita Whisper > "My words to spell correctly".
Add names, companies and places you say often, one per line,
save the file, then restart Anita Whisper.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Your voice is turned into text on this computer. Nothing you say is sent
over the internet, and no account is needed.

NEEDS
Windows 10 or 11 and a microphone.
