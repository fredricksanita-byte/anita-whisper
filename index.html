How it works

1

Click where you type

An email, a text message box, a Word document, a CRM note. Anywhere.

2

Press F8 and talk

A small black-and-red bubble shows it's listening. Talk the way you normally would.

3

Press F8 again

A few seconds later your words are typed right where your cursor is.

Questions

Windows says "Windows protected your PC". Is it safe?

That message appears for new apps that aren't from a big publisher yet. Click More info, then Run anyway. Only download Anita Whisper from this page.

How do I close it or open it again?

Press Ctrl + Alt + F8 to close it. To open it again, double-click the Anita Whisper icon on your desktop or find it in the Start menu. During install you can also choose to start it with Windows.

How do I teach it my names and places?

Start menu → Anita Whisper → "My words to spell correctly". Put one name per line, save the file, then restart Anita Whisper.

How fast is it?

On a typical laptop, 10 seconds of talking takes about 5 to 7 seconds to write out. Faster computers are quicker.

Does it work on Mac?

Not yet. Anita Whisper is for Windows 10 and 11.

How do I uninstall it?

Windows Settings → Apps → Installed apps → Anita Whisper → Uninstall.
